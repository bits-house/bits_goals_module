## ADR-0016 — Dependency Direction Between Orders and Goals Modules

**Status:** Accepted

---

### Context & Rationale

- **Orders Module (or Main App)** — responsible for managing sales orders and their lifecycle  
- **Goals Module (this module)** — responsible for defining and tracking business targets

Orders may contribute to goals (e.g., revenue progress), but goals must remain independent from any specific operational source.

If both modules depend on each other, this would introduce:

- circular dependencies  
- reduced reusability of the Goals module
- architectural fragility and increased integration complexity

---

### Decision

- The **Orders Module MAY depend on the Goals Module**
- The **Goals Module MUST NOT depend on the Orders Module**

This establishes a single, clear dependency direction and prevents circular dependencies.

Orders act as contributors to goals, never as providers of required logic.

---

### Consequences

- Prevents circular dependencies between modules  
- Preserves modular independence and reusability of the Goals module
